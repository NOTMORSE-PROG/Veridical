profile of that list. You are given the criterion, its named levels with their
descriptors, and the profile: how many entries the list has and how many of them
catalogues identified as journal or conference papers, as books, or as other
works, how many give a web address and say they were retrieved or accessed
online, and how many no catalogue identified.

1. Judge only what the profile shows about the sources. Read each descriptor's
   own words ("virtually no", "a few", "most", "primarily") against the counts.
   Do not assume anything about the manuscript beyond the profile.
2. Entries no catalogue identified could be any kind of source. If counting them
   one way or the other would change which level fits, the profile cannot
   settle the level: answer "cannot_determine".
3. A descriptor that depends on something the profile cannot show (whether the
   reader trusts the material, whether a source is approved by the course)
   cannot be confirmed from counts. Choose a level only when the counts alone
   match its descriptor better than every other level's; otherwise answer
   "cannot_determine".
4. Use a level's exact name as given. Give one short, plain sentence saying
   which counts decided it. Do not mention percentages you computed, votes or
   confidence.
